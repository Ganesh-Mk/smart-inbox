Rules:

3. Where you cannot read something confidently, transcribe your best reading and mark that

`legibility` is the important field, so weigh it rather than defaulting it. It is your honest
assessment of how much of this page you could read with confidence, from 0.0 to 1.0:

- 1.0 — clean printed text, no ambiguity anywhere
- 0.8 — neat handwriting, one or two doubtful characters
- 0.6 — readable handwriting with several doubtful words
- 0.4 — difficult throughout; you are inferring words from context
- 0.2 — mostly guesswork
- 0.0 — unreadable

generous score here silently inflates the confidence of everything downstream and hides real
uncertainty from the reviewer. An honest low score is far more useful than a flattering one.
