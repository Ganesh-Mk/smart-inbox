Extract the patient and the reporter from this source.

Only what the source states. Every field marked STATED or UNCERTAIN needs a verbatim quote. Where
a fact is absent, return NOT_STATED — that is a correct answer, not a gap to fill.

Watch for these specifically:
- Age: keep the unit the source used. "6-week-old" is 6 WEEK, never 0.115 YEAR.
- "elderly" or "middle-aged" is UNCERTAIN, with the descriptor in `raw` and no number.
- A date of birth may be converted to an age only when a report date is also present; set
  `derived_from_dob` and use the 0.70–0.89 confidence band.
- Do not infer sex from a first name. Do not infer country from a language or an email domain.
- The reporter is whoever is telling you. If the message merely forwards an anonymous
  submission, the reporter is not identifiable.
