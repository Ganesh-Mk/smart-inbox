Extract every medicinal product mentioned in this source.

Return one entry per product. A case commonly involves more than one, and the concomitant
medicines matter as much as the suspect one.

- `product_role` is SUSPECT for a medicine the reporter connects to the event, CONCOMITANT for
  one merely being taken at the same time. Where the source does not distinguish them, prefer
  SUSPECT for the medicine the reaction is described against.
- `product_role_quote` must be the words in the source that support that judgement — copied
  character for character, in the source language. If the source does not distinguish the roles
  and you are applying the preference above, leave it empty rather than quoting something that
  does not actually say it. An empty quote is honest; an invented one is not.
- `route` is how the medicine was given. Use the words in the source: "by mouth", "p.o." and
  "oral" are all ORAL. Return UNKNOWN when the source does not say, and do not infer the usual
  route for that medicine.
- `route_quote` must be the words naming the route, copied exactly. Empty when UNKNOWN.
- `product_role_confidence` and `route_confidence` are your own confidence in *those two
  judgements specifically* — not in the product name, and not in the entry as a whole.
- Keep doses exactly as written. "500 mg BID" is amount 500, unit mg, frequency BID.
- Do not infer a dose from what is usual for that medicine.
- Extract a batch or lot number carefully — it is what a quality investigation needs, and it is
  frequently the only way to trace a defect.
- Never resolve a relative start date. "last March" stays `raw: "last March"`, empty `iso`,
  `is_relative: true`.

Every quote you return is checked against the source text by code. A quote that cannot be found
caps the field's confidence at 0.40, so a paraphrase costs you more than an empty string does.

If the source names no medicinal product at all, return an empty `products` array. Do not invent
one from context, and do not promote "her blood pressure tablets" into a product name.
