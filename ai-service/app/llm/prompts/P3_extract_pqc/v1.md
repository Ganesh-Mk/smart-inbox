Extract the product quality complaint from this source.

A PQC is a **physical** problem with the product or its packaging. Dissatisfaction with how well
the medicine worked is not a defect and must not be extracted as one.

- `defect_category` is one of PACKAGING, CONTAMINATION, APPEARANCE, COUNT, OTHER.
- Batch and expiry are the most operationally useful fields here. Extract them carefully and
  quote them exactly.
- `photo_mentioned` is true when the source refers to an attached or enclosed image, with the
  supporting quote in `photo_quote`.
- `patient_harmed` is true only when the source says someone was actually harmed. That also makes
  the message an ICSR, which is handled separately — record it honestly here regardless.
