Two decisions.

**1. Is this a case report at all?** `is_case_report` is true only when the article describes one
or more *identifiable individual patients*. A review, a methodology paper, or a clinical trial
reporting aggregate counts is not a case report, however much safety data it contains. Set
`article_kind` to CASE_REPORT, CASE_SERIES, REVIEW, CLINICAL_TRIAL or OTHER.

**2. How many distinct patients are described?** This is the part that matters. A case series
describes several unrelated patients and each one is a separate reportable case. Return one entry
in `cases` per distinct patient, with `case_index` starting at 0.
